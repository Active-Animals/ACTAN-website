Welcome to Active Animals. If you're interested in classifying animal behaviour, then make yourself at home.

## Behaviour Ontology (ACTAN)

On the following pages, you can find an [INTRODUCTION](ontology/index) to the ontology (a kind of uber-glossary). Its [SCOPING](ontology/scoping) page reflects on the need for an ontology and its potted history. 

The ontology itself hosts a plethora of labels, synonyms, definitions, and other provenance or explanatory information. The [Alpha-3 version](https://webprotege.stanford.edu/#projects/a0eaa10e-bc54-4a59-94e3-529063fe71c6) is hosted at WebProtégé and is open for viewing after registering your username. (We have provided some [notes here](https://active-animals.github.io/ACTAN-website/ontology/editing#Using-WebProtégé) on using WebProtégé.)

The latest version of it is searchable, and its internal hierarchy and connections viewed, at [Bioportal](https://purl.bioontology.org/ontology/ACTAN?conceptid=http%3A%2F%2Fpurl.obolibrary.org%2Fobo%2FACTAN_0000002).

If you're keen on collaborating to develop the **technology** processes for it, to curate its **content**, you wish to **stress-test** the ontology's labels against your observations, or to support its **public** facies such as this website or at Bioportal, take a look at the INTRODUCTION section: [Can I contribute?](https://active-animals.github.io/ACTAN-website/ontology/index#can-i-contribute)

## Behaviour Labels

Most users will not come here to look at the detailed ontology though. They will want to skim through a more simple catalogue for labels to apply to behaviours they have observed and recorded, perhaps as photos, videos, or recordings. See the full [CHECKLIST](https://active-animals.github.io/ACTAN-website/ontology/checklist) of ACTAN behaviours for quick reference.

## Observation Repositories

#### iNaturalist

The [Active Animals (AA)](https://www.inaturalist.org/projects/active-animals-aa) project at iNaturalist is a way for animal behavioural records to be aggregated systematically there. Still in its Alpha phase, it can be viewed by anyone, but new membership is limited to stress-testers.
